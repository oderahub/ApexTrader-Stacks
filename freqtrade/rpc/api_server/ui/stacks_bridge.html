How It Works

  1. Bridge USDC - Use Hermes to convert USDC to USDCx on Stacks
  2. Connect Wallet - Connect your Leather/Hiro wallet above
  3. Deposit to Escrow - Transfer USDCx to the escrow smart contract
  4. Authorize Bot - Allow ApexTrader to execute trades on your behalf
  5. Auto-Trade - Bot trades within your escrow limits
  6. Withdraw - Reclaim your funds from escrow anytime
